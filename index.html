Because sometimes, the greatest thing someone can give you isn't a gift or anything material—it's their presence when you need it most. And you gave me yours. 🤍

This became one of the best birthday gifts I've ever received—not because it was expensive or grand, but because you made it with your own hands and gave it with your heart. Thank you for that. I love you so much. ❤️

I love you so, so much, and here's to all the memories we've made and all the ones still waiting for us. 🤍
